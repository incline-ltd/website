The guardrail path

A guarded path from idea to live.

  1. Describe

    The strategy starts as plain words — the AI drafts entry, risk, and execution rules you can read.

  2. Backtest

    Historical results show win rate, drawdown, and profit factor before you choose a deployment mode.

  3. Paper trade

    The strategy runs on live markets with no money at risk, building a track record you can audit.

  4. Approve

    Choose approval mode when every proposed order should wait for an explicit Telegram confirmation.

  5. Run, guarded

    Throttles, exposure caps, and a full audit trail — live means watched, never fire-and-forget.
