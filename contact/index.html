Before you write

Common questions.

What happens when I try this form?

Your browser checks the fields and shows a local confirmation. This example has no submission service and does not send or save your details. Use the live website to send an actual enquiry.

What should I include in the first message?

The workflow as it runs today, who touches it, where it breaks, and what a trustworthy result looks like. Rough words are fine — that is what the first call is for.

Do you work with the tools we already use?

Yes. Agents and tools get wired to what you already run — APIs, databases, dashboards, Telegram — rather than replacing your stack.

How does AI fit into the build?

AI drafts and accelerates. Sensitive actions can require human approval; direct automation follows the controls agreed for that workflow.

Who owns what you build?

You do. The code is handed over readable and documented. We can keep operating it after handoff, or hand it off completely — your call.

Do you take small projects?

If the loop is complete and genuinely useful, size does not matter. Describe it and you will get an honest answer about what it needs.
